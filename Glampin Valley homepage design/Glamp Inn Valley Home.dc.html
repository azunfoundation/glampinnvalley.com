The Valley

Perched high above the world, surrounded by an endless valley and a verdant forest.

As you ascend the winding roads into the Vikarabad hills, anticipation builds. Your futuristic yet intimate abode waits on the ridge: a geodesic dome with a private deck, an infinity pool at the edge of the forest, and evenings that end around a fire under a sky full of stars.

Glamp Inn Valley is Hyderabad's first stay of its kind — an eco-friendly haven where luxury finds its home amidst nature, and where peacocks are your only neighbours.

Getting here

Leave the city after lunch. Watch the sunset from your deck.

Glamp Inn Valley sits in the Vikarabad hills west of Hyderabad, roughly two hours by road. The drive itself is part of the escape: past Chevella, into Vikarabad, then a red-earth road through the trees to Thirmalapur.
